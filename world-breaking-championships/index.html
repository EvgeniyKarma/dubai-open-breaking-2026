  1. The number of athletes per crew is fixed: each crew must consist of five athletes.

  2. The Championships are held in the format of qualifying rounds. In the preliminary round (preselection) the judging is «closed by points». The points awarded by the judges are added together for each athlete or crew to determine the final ranking and placing in the standings.

  3. In the qualification rounds (battles), the knock-out phase, the Small Final and the Final, the judging is «open by points». The athlete or crew that wins the most rounds in a battle becomes the winner of that battle, including in the Final.

  4. If the participants in a battle win the same number of rounds, an additional round is held to determine the winner of the battle.

  5. Battle format for solo competitors (1 vs 1):

    Preselection1 round
    Top 322 rounds (best of three)
    Top 162 rounds (best of three)
    Top 82 rounds (best of three)
    Semi-finals2 rounds (best of three)
    Final3 rounds

  6. Battle format for crews (5 vs 5):

    Preselection5 rounds (1 routine)
    Top 165 rounds (2 routines)
    Top 85 rounds (3 routines)
    Semi-finals7 rounds (4 routines)
    Final10 rounds (5 routines)
